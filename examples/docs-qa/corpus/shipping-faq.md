# Shipping FAQ

Answers to the questions customer support hears most often about shipping
Acorn Robotics hardware orders.

## Processing time

Orders ship within three business days of payment clearing, not from the
order date -- a payment placed on a Friday evening typically clears
Monday, so the three-day clock starts then. Custom configurations (a
non-standard sensor package, for example) add two additional business
days for assembly before the shipping clock starts.

## Delivery estimates

Domestic delivery is five to seven business days after shipping via
standard ground; expedited air shipping is one to two business days for an
additional fee shown at checkout. International delivery varies by
destination and customs processing, typically seven to fourteen business
days, and the estimate shown at checkout already accounts for the
destination country's typical customs delay.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed automatically the moment a label is printed,
which is usually the same day the order ships. If a tracking number shows
no movement for more than three business days, contact support with the
order number rather than the tracking number, since support needs the
order number to look up the underlying carrier account.

## Damaged or lost shipments

Report a damaged shipment within seven days of delivery with photos of the
damage and the packaging; a replacement ships immediately once photos are
received, without waiting for a carrier investigation to conclude. A
shipment marked delivered but not received is treated as lost after five
business days of no resolution with the carrier, at which point a
replacement or refund is offered.

## International duties and taxes

Import duties and taxes are calculated at checkout for supported
countries and included in the order total; for unsupported countries, the
carrier collects duties on delivery and the customer is responsible for
that amount separately from the order total.

## Returns

Hardware may be returned unused, in original packaging, within thirty days
of delivery for a full refund minus the original shipping cost. A used or
opened unit is only eligible for return if it is defective, verified
through the standard return merchandise authorization process.

## Common questions

**How long until my order ships?** Within three business days of payment
clearing, plus two more for a custom configuration.

**What do I do if my tracking number hasn't moved in a while?** Contact
support with your order number after three business days of no movement.

**Can I return an opened unit?** Only if it is defective and goes through
the return merchandise authorization process.
