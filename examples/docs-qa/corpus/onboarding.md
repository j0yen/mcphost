# New hire onboarding

Welcome to Acorn Robotics. This page walks a new hire through the first two
weeks: accounts, paperwork, equipment, and the buddy program.

## Day one

Your manager sends a calendar invite for a 30-minute welcome call before
your start date. On day one, IT provisions your laptop, your badge, and
your accounts in the identity provider. You will receive a temporary
password by text message, not email, since your email account does not
exist until the identity provider finishes provisioning it (usually within
one hour of your badge being activated at the front desk).

## Paperwork deadline

New hires have five business days from their start date to complete
onboarding paperwork: the W-4 or local tax equivalent, direct deposit
details, the equipment agreement, and the code of conduct acknowledgement.
Payroll will not run your first paycheck until every document in that list
is signed. HR sends two reminder emails, on day three and day four, before
escalating an incomplete packet to your manager on day six.

## The buddy program

Every new hire is paired with a buddy from a different team, not their own
reporting line, for the first thirty days. The buddy's job is answering the
questions that feel too small for a manager: where the good coffee is,
which Slack channels matter, and how to file an expense report without
guessing. Buddies are volunteers; sign up in the #onboarding-buddies
channel if you want to be one for a future hire.

## Equipment

Standard equipment is a laptop, a monitor, and a headset, shipped to your
home address five business days before your start date if you are remote,
or waiting at your desk if you are on-site. Special equipment requests
(a second monitor, a different keyboard layout, an ergonomic chair) go
through the #it-requests channel and typically take one additional week.

## First-week checklist

- Complete onboarding paperwork within five business days.
- Meet your buddy and your skip-level manager.
- Set up two-factor authentication on your identity provider account.
- Join your team's standing meetings and the all-hands calendar.
- Read the security basics page and the expense policy page.

## Thirty/sixty/ninety

Your manager schedules three check-ins: at thirty days (are the basics
working?), sixty days (are you contributing to real work?), and ninety
days (is this a good fit for both sides?). These are conversations, not
performance reviews; the first performance review happens at the six-month
mark, aligned to the standard review cycle every other employee uses.

## Common questions

**When does my email account start working?** Usually within an hour of
badge activation, but allow until the end of day one before filing a
ticket.

**What if I miss the paperwork deadline?** Contact HR immediately; a short
extension is normal for genuine blockers (a delayed government ID, for
example), but silence past day six triggers a manager escalation.

**Can I pick my own buddy?** No, buddies are assigned to make sure the
program covers people who wouldn't otherwise get one, but you are welcome
to keep talking to your buddy long after the first thirty days end.
