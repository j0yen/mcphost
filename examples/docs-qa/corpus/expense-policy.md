# Expense reimbursement policy

This page covers what Acorn Robotics reimburses, how to submit a claim, and
how long approval takes.

## What is reimbursable

Reasonable business expenses are reimbursable: travel (flights, trains,
mileage at the published rate), lodging, meals while traveling, client
entertainment with a business purpose noted, conference and training fees
approved in advance, and home-office supplies up to the annual allowance.
Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a client meal, capped at two drinks
per attendee.

## Meal limits

Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to fifty dollars per day, itemized
by breakfast, lunch, and dinner. A meal under twenty-five dollars does not
require an itemized receipt; a credit card statement line is enough. A meal
of twenty-five dollars or more requires the itemized receipt showing what
was ordered, not just the total, because tax auditors ask for it during the
annual review.

## Submitting a claim

Claims go through the expenses tool, not email or Slack. Attach a receipt
image for every line over twenty-five dollars, choose a cost center, and
add a one-sentence business purpose. Claims older than ninety days from the
expense date are not reimbursed except by an explicit finance exception,
which requires a director's approval.

## Approval and payment

Your manager approves first, then finance does a spot audit on about one in
five claims before payment. Approved claims are paid on the next payroll
cycle after approval, which is at most two weeks after submission if you
submit promptly. Rejected claims come back with a reason and can be
resubmitted once corrected.

## Mileage and personal car use

Mileage is reimbursed at the current published rate per mile for
business travel in a personal vehicle, calculated from your normal
workplace, not your home, unless you are working from home that day.
Parking and tolls are reimbursed separately with a receipt; a parking
ticket or fine is never reimbursable.

## Home office allowance

Remote employees get an annual allowance for home-office supplies: a chair,
a desk, a monitor arm, or similar. The allowance resets every January 1st
and does not roll over if unused. Equipment bought under this allowance
belongs to the employee, unlike laptops and monitors shipped by IT, which
remain company property and must be returned on departure.

## Common questions

**Do I need a receipt for a five-dollar coffee?** No, anything under
twenty-five dollars just needs the card statement line.

**What happens if I submit a claim after ninety days?** It is rejected by
default; ask your manager to request a finance exception if there was a
genuine delay outside your control.

**Are personal phone bills reimbursable?** Only the business-use portion,
and only with a manager's prior approval noted in the claim.
