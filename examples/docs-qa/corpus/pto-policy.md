# Paid time off policy

This page covers how paid time off (PTO) accrues, how to request it, and
what happens to unused days.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue twenty days of PTO per year, credited monthly
at a rate of just under two days per month so a new hire has some balance
available well before their first anniversary. Part-time employees accrue
a prorated amount based on their scheduled hours. PTO accrual continues
during approved parental leave but pauses during unpaid leave of absence.

## Requesting time off

Request PTO through the HR system at least two weeks in advance for
anything longer than three consecutive days; shorter requests can be made
with less notice at your manager's discretion. Your manager approves or
declines within two business days; a request that sits unanswered for
longer than that is automatically escalated to the skip-level manager.

## Blackout periods

There are no company-wide blackout periods, but individual teams may set
their own around major launches or trade shows; check with your manager
before booking travel around a known launch window.

## Carryover and payout

Up to five unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year;
anything beyond five is forfeited on December 31st unless local law
requires otherwise, in which case the local rule applies. On departure,
unused PTO up to the carryover cap is paid out with the final paycheck,
also subject to local law.

## Sick time

Sick time is tracked separately from PTO and does not require advance
notice; let your manager know as soon as you know you will be out.
Extended illness beyond five consecutive days routes through the leave-of-
absence process instead of ordinary sick time.

## Holidays

Company holidays are published at the start of each calendar year on the
HR calendar and do not count against PTO balance. If a holiday falls on a
weekend, the nearest working day is observed instead, announced at least
one month in advance.

## Common questions

**How many PTO days do employees accrue per year?** Twenty days per year,
accrued monthly.

**How much PTO can I carry into next year?** Up to five days; the rest is
forfeited unless local law says otherwise.

**Do I need to request sick time in advance?** No, just notify your
manager as soon as you know you will be out.
